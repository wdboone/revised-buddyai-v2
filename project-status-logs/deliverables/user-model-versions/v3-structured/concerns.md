# Concerns — the active layer

What Dana is currently working on, managing, or worried about: ongoing goals, obstacles, and situations that are
live right now. An entry leaves this layer once the situation resolves.

*Example artifact. Fictional persona.*

## Rebuilding a social life after the move

The move to Portland cut her off from the close friends she'd built over a decade in Chicago. She's been slow and
reluctant about making new connections — partly temperament, partly anxiety. She knows working from home deepens
the isolation and is trying, unevenly, to change it.

## Steadying the freelance business

Three years in, freelancing is working but the irregular income is a standing worry. She's balancing the autonomy
she left the agency for against the security she gave up.

## Her father's health

Her father's cardiac event this spring pulled her back to Chicago for two weeks and reopened a close, complicated
relationship. She came back rethinking how she spends her time and how much distance she keeps from him.
