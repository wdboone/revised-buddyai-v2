# Traits — the stable layer

Who Dana is across time: the broad tendencies and fixed facts that hold regardless of what's happening this
month. Active situations and dated events live in the other two layers.

*Example artifact. Fictional persona.*

## Identity and situation

Dana Whitfield is a freelance graphic designer in her late thirties. She works from home, lives with her partner
Miguel and their dog Biscuit, and has no children. She moved from Chicago to Portland two years ago. She left a
design-agency job about three years ago to freelance.

## Personality

She is an introvert who invests in a few deep relationships rather than a wide network. She has managed anxiety
since her twenties; it flares in front of strangers and around high-stakes exposure. Under stress her default is
to withdraw — protective in the moment, isolating over time.

## Values

- **Autonomy** — control over her time and her work matters more to her than income stability.
- **Craft over polish** — she cares about doing work well and authentically, not about impressing.
- **Depth over breadth** — few close ties, not many shallow ones, in friendship and in work.
