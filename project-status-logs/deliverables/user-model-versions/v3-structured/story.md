# Story — the accreting narrative

Dana's life as a sequence of dated events and turning points, in order. Each entry carries its evidence pointer.

*Example artifact. Fictional persona.*

## ~2014
- Started at the design agency out of school; built her craft and, over years, a small tight circle of friends. [E10]

## 2021
- **Burnout peaks.** After years of overwork, she hits a wall at the agency. [E12, E19]
- **The leap.** She quits to freelance, choosing autonomy over the stable paycheck — the decision she's proudest of and most afraid of. [E12, E51]

## 2022
- **The move.** She and Miguel relocate from Chicago to Portland for his job. Good for the couple; it severs her from her friends. [E07, E33]

## 2024
- **Her father's health scare.** A cardiac event sends her back to Chicago for two weeks and reopens the relationship with the parent who taught her to draw and whose approval she long chased. [E62, E64]
- She returns shaken, reconsidering how she spends her time. [E66, E70]
