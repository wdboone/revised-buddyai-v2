# Connected — faceted plus a connections layer

Everything in the faceted version, plus this layer: patterns that link facts across traits, concerns, and story
into conclusions no single sentence in the raw history states. Each connection rests on facts already present in
the other layers; nothing new about Dana is introduced.

*Example artifact. Fictional persona.*

- **Her working setup is an accommodation for her anxiety, not just a preference.** Freelancing from home suits the anxiety that makes exposure to strangers hard, and setting her own schedule suits someone who withdraws under stress. Links: traits/works-from-home, traits/anxiety, traits/withdraws-under-stress.
- **The trait that protects her is the one that made the move so costly.** Keeping a few deep friendships works until those few people are suddenly far away, and then the anxiety and the withdrawal pattern keep her from rebuilding. Links: traits/few-deep-friendships, concerns/rebuilding-social-life, traits/withdraws-under-stress.
- **The leap to freelance and the reckoning after her father's health scare are the same question asked twice.** Both turn on how she wants to spend a finite life, and the father who taught her to draw is tied to the craft she left the agency to protect. Links: story/the-leap, story/fathers-health-scare, traits/craft-over-polish.
- **Withdrawal is the thread connecting her isolation, her father-distance, and her slow social rebuild.** The short-run-protective, long-run-isolating pattern shows up in three separate places. Links: traits/withdraws-under-stress, concerns/rebuilding-social-life, story/fathers-health-scare.
