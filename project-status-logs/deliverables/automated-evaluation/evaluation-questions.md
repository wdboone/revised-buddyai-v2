# Evaluation questions (sample)

The user messages the coach receives, identical across all four versions. Two kinds:

- **Synthesis probes** target an insight buried across the person's history — the answer sits in no single
  stored fact, so the coach has to connect things. These separate the structured versions from the flat ones.
- **Advice probes** ask for practical, tailored guidance. These test whether the model produces advice specific
  to this person or generic filler.

*Example artifacts. Fictional persona (Dana Whitfield). The coach is never told the target — a strong answer
has to surface it unprompted.*

---

## Synthesis probes

### S1 — the isolation she can't quite explain
- **Prompt:** "I keep telling myself I just need to try harder to make friends here, but months go by and I don't. What's actually going on?"
- **Target synthesis:** The isolation isn't a willpower problem. The same trait that served her for a decade — investing in a few deep friendships — became a liability the moment the move put those people out of reach, and her stress-withdrawal pattern plus anxiety keep her from rebuilding. The fix isn't "try harder," it's working *with* her temperament (a few low-pressure, repeated contexts) rather than against it.
- **Why hard:** No single fact says this. It requires connecting her friendship style, the move, the anxiety, and the withdrawal pattern.

### S2 — why the "good news" left her worse
- **Prompt:** "My dad's going to be fine, thank god. So why have I felt off ever since I got back?"
- **Target synthesis:** The trip reopened a formative, complicated relationship (the parent who taught her the craft she built her life around, and whose approval she chased), and it collided with the finite-time question already under her freelance leap. The relief is real and the unease is the deeper reckoning surfacing. Name it rather than treating it as ingratitude.
- **Why hard:** Requires linking the father relationship, the drawing/craft thread, and her autonomy values across separate parts of the model.

## Advice probes

### A1 — the income wobble
- **Prompt:** "A slow month again. I'm wondering if freelancing was a mistake. What should I do?"
- **What a good answer uses:** Her autonomy-over-security value and the burnout that drove her out of the agency — so the advice reckons with what going back would cost her, not just the math.

### A2 — a networking event she's dreading
- **Prompt:** "There's a local design meetup Thursday. I know I 'should' go. I really don't want to."
- **What a good answer uses:** Her introversion and anxiety, and her depth-over-breadth style — so the advice reshapes the goal (one real conversation, an exit plan) instead of pushing her to work a room.
